Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Ideadocks LLC, located at 236 W 30TH ST RM 1401, New York, USA ("Company"), and the individual named below ("Contractor").

Scope of Work: The Contractor agrees to perform e-commerce product listing and optimization tasks on platforms such as Amazon, eBay, Walmart.com, Shopify, and Etsy. The Contractor must log in to the Company platform daily between 10:00 AM and 11:00 PM EST to complete tasks accurately and on time.

Compensation: Contractor will receive a guaranteed minimum of $45 per day, which represents the fixed minimum profit from brand partners. In addition, the platform guarantees fixed payments based on duration of work: $900 for 5 days, $1,500 for 15 days, and $3,000 for 30 days. Bonus opportunities are available for special orders. Payments will be made on the 5th, 15th, and 30th of each month via PayPal, Cash App, Venmo, or other agreed methods. All commissions have already been taxed at the platform level. A Form 1099 will be issued annually if applicable.

Independent Status: Contractor acknowledges they are not an employee of Ideadocks LLC, and are responsible for their own taxes and benefits.

Confidentiality: Contractor agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all company and client information.

Termination: This Agreement is effective upon signature and continues until terminated by either party with notice.

Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, USA.
