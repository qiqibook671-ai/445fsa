Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of [Date], by and between Ideadocks LLC, a New York limited liability company (“Company”), located at 236 W 30th St RM 1401, New York, NY, USA, and [Contractor Name] (“Contractor”), collectively referred to as the “Parties.”

1. Scope of Work

The Contractor agrees to perform e-commerce product listing and data upload services (“Services”) for the Company. This includes uploading product data and detail pages for brand partners such as Estée Lauder, Audemars Piguet, Armani, Ferragamo, and others to various online platforms including Amazon, eBay, Walmart.com, Shopify, and Etsy. The Contractor shall ensure all product information is accurate, error-free, and uploaded on schedule.

The Contractor shall log in daily to the Company’s official platform between 10 AM and 11 PM EST to complete assigned tasks.

2. Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor acknowledges and agrees that they are an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Company. The Contractor shall have no authority to bind or obligate the Company in any manner.

3. Compensation

3.1 Base Salary and Task-Based Earnings

  • The Contractor will earn a guaranteed minimum of $45 daily upon completion of two task sets.
  • Wages will be calculated based on completed orders and paid as a fixed salary on the 5th, 15th, and 30th of each month.
  • Additional earnings may be earned from special bonus orders, holiday bonuses, and the platform’s anniversary bonuses.

3.2 Payment Methods

Payments will be made via PayPal, Cash App, Crypto.com, Onchain, Venmo, or other mutually agreed methods. International transfers may take 3 to 7 business days depending on processing times.

3.3 Taxes

The commissions paid have been taxed at the platform level. After one full month of work, the Company will provide a 1099 tax form for the Contractor’s records.

4. Term and Termination

This Agreement will commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated by either Party with seven (7) days written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for cause.

5. Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information, trade secrets, and data obtained during the engagement. The Contractor shall not disclose or use any such information except as necessary to perform the Services.

6. Intellectual Property

All product data, listings, and related materials created or uploaded by the Contractor for the Company’s clients shall be the exclusive property of the Company.

7. Miscellaneous

  • This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations or agreements.
  • Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
  • This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
